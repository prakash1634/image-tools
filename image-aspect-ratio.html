Free Image Aspect Ratio Calculator

ImageTools Image Aspect Ratio Calculator helps you find the exact width, height and aspect ratio of an image. Upload a JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP image and the tool automatically calculates its dimensions and ratio.

What Is Image Aspect Ratio?

Aspect ratio describes the proportional relationship between an image's width and height. Common ratios include 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 4:5 and 9:16.

How to Check an Image Aspect Ratio

  1. Tap Choose an Image.
  2. Select the image from your device.
  3. The width and height will be calculated automatically.
  4. View the exact and common aspect ratio.
  5. Tap Clear Image when finished.

Common Image Aspect Ratios

  • 1:1 — Square images
  • 4:3 — Common photo and display ratio
  • 3:2 — Common photography ratio
  • 16:9 — Widescreen images and video
  • 9:16 — Vertical mobile content
  • 4:5 — Common portrait format

Browser-Based Processing

Image dimensions and aspect ratio are calculated directly in your browser. No image-processing server is required for this tool.
