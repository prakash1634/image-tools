1. Tap the Choose Image button and select your JPG, JPEG or PNG image.

Does image compression reduce file size?

Yes. Image compression can reduce the file size of an image, making it easier to store, share and upload online.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

The compression is performed directly in your browser. The tool does not need to upload your image to a server for the compression process.
