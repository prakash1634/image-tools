What Is an Inverted Image?

An inverted image replaces each color with its opposite value. Bright areas become dark and dark areas become bright, creating a negative-style visual effect.

Why Use an Image Color Inverter?

Color inversion can be useful for creative photo effects, graphic design experiments, visual testing, and creating negative-style images.
