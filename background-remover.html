How to Remove an Image Background

1. Tap Choose an Image and select a JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP image.

2. Tap Remove Background and wait while the AI processes your image.

3. Preview the image with the background removed.

4. Tap Download PNG to save the transparent image.

Is this AI background remover free?

Yes. ImageTools provides this background remover free of charge. The tool runs in your browser and creates a transparent PNG image.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

The image processing is designed to run in your browser. Your selected image does not need to be uploaded to an ImageTools server.

Which image formats are supported?

You can select JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images for background removal. The result is provided as a PNG image with transparency.

Why use a transparent PNG?

A transparent PNG can be useful for product images, profile pictures, logos, thumbnails, designs and other graphics where you want to remove the original background.
