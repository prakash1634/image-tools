How to Remove a Background

1. Tap Choose Image and select your photo.

2. Adjust Background Tolerance if needed.

3. Tap Remove Background.

4. Tap Download Transparent PNG to save the result.

Is this background remover free?

Yes. This basic background remover is free and processes the image directly in your browser.

Best results are achieved with simple, solid-color backgrounds.
