Add Text to Image Online Free

ImageTools Image Text Generator lets you add custom text to an image directly in your browser. Upload an image, enter your text, choose a font size and text color, preview the result, and download your finished image.

How to Add Text to an Image

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Enter the text you want to add.
  3. Set the font size.
  4. Choose your preferred text color.
  5. Preview the image with your text.
  6. Tap Download Image to save it.

Customize Text on Your Image

You can change the text, font size, and text color to create a simple custom graphic. The text is placed in the center of the image.

What Can You Use It For?

Adding text to images can be useful for social media posts, thumbnails, simple banners, announcements, quotes, captions, personal graphics, and other creative projects.

Browser-Based Image Editing

The text is added using HTML5 Canvas directly in your browser. No separate image-editing application is required for this basic text overlay.

Supported Images

The tool accepts common browser-supported image formats such as JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP and other image types supported by your browser.
