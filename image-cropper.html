How to Crop an Image

1. Tap the Choose Image button and select your JPG, JPEG or PNG image.

2. Enter the X and Y position and the crop width and height.

3. Tap Crop Image to create the cropped image.

4. Tap Download Cropped Image to save it.

Is this image cropper free?

Yes. This online image cropper is free and works directly in your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.
